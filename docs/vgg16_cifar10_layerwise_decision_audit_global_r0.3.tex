No: S9-global-r0.3 does not mean all nine methods agreed with each other at 90\%. The threshold is applied to pairwise rank correlation, with prune-set overlap also required. Methods without a passing simple proxy are retained as complementary contributors, which is why a stack can be large even when internal agreement is sparse.
